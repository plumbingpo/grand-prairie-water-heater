How Arlington Homeowners Can Extend Water Heater Life

Routine maintenance is the difference between a water heater that runs for years and one that floods the garage in year seven. Our advice for homeowners is a short list: a yearly flush, an anode inspection every few years, a yearly test of the T&P valve, a temperature setting around 120 degrees, and yearly descaling for any tankless unit on hard water, so problems are found while they're still inexpensive.

Age matters too. Most tank water heaters run 8 to 12 years, tankless units often reach 20 with regular descaling, and heat pump models usually last 10 to 15. If your heater is past that window and showing warning signs such as rusty hot water, rumbling during heating, moisture around the base, or showers that go cold sooner, a planned replacement is nearly always less expensive and much easier on you than an emergency one after the tank lets go.

Small problems rarely stay small when they're ignored. Consider a spent anode rod: replacing it is a short, affordable repair, but once it's gone, the water starts eating the tank itself, and suddenly a minor fix has become a full replacement. That's why we ask homeowners to save our number in their phone and call at the first sign of trouble. Catching it early saves money and prevents a surprise cleanup.

What Goes Wrong With Local Water Heaters

Every type of water heater has its own weak points, and we work on all of them in Arlington. Gas tanks tend to have trouble with thermocouples and flame sensors, dirty burners, and draft problems in the flue. Electric units burn out heating elements and thermostats, particularly when sediment buries the lower element. Tankless heaters throw error codes for scale buildup, ignition faults, and flow sensor problems, and many of those trace back to skipped descaling. Hybrid heat pump units depend on a clean filter and a free-flowing condensate line, and they don't do well in small, closed-in spaces. We service all four types regularly and stock the common parts to finish most repairs in one trip.
