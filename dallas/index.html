How Dallas Homeowners Can Extend Water Heater Life

The best way to avoid a surprise leak is routine maintenance. We recommend that homeowners flush the tank once a year (more often with hard water), have the anode rod checked every three to five years, lift the T&P valve lever once a year to make sure it isn't stuck, and keep the thermostat around 120 degrees, which limits scalding risk and slows mineral buildup. Tankless owners should plan on a descaling flush once a year in hard-water areas.

Equipment age plays a big role. With regular service, a tank water heater typically lasts 8 to 12 years, a heat pump water heater 10 to 15, and a tankless unit up to 20. Once a heater passes that range and starts acting up, like discolored hot water, popping noises, or moisture around the base, it's smart to start planning a replacement. Replacing on your own schedule costs less and is far less stressful than scrambling for a new heater after a leak.

Minor issues don't stay minor for long. Consider a spent anode rod: swapping it out is a quick, inexpensive repair, but once it's gone, the water begins corroding the tank steel, and just like that a minor fix becomes a full replacement. That's why we ask homeowners to keep our number in their phone and call at the first sign of trouble. Catching it early saves time and money and spares you a surprise cleanup.

Common Water Heater Problems in Dallas

Every type of water heater has its own weak points, and we work on all of them around Dallas. Gas tanks usually give trouble through pilot assemblies and thermocouples, dirty burners, and venting that doesn't draft. Electric tanks lose heating elements and thermostats, particularly when sediment buries the lower element. Tankless units throw error codes for scale buildup, ignition faults, and flow sensor problems, most of which trace back to skipped descaling. Heat pump water heaters need clean air filters and a clear condensate drain, and they struggle in cramped closets. We service all four types regularly and stock the common parts to finish most repairs in one trip.
